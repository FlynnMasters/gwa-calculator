What is General Weighted Average (GWA)?

General Weighted Average or GWA is a numerical representation of a student's overall academic performance. Unlike a regular average, GWA considers both grades and subject units, making it more accurate for evaluating academic standing.

How GWA is Computed

Why Monitoring Your GWA Matters

Tracking your GWA regularly helps students stay aware of academic performance and eligibility for scholarships, dean’s list recognition, retention policies, and Latin honors such as Cum Laude or Magna Cum Laude.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is GWA different from GPA?

Yes. GWA is commonly used in the Philippines, while GPA is more common internationally. Different schools may also use different conversion systems.

How often should students compute GWA?

Students should compute their GWA every semester to monitor academic standing and scholarship eligibility.

Do all universities use the same grading system?

No. Some universities have slight differences in grading scales and computation methods.

Can this calculator be used for college and senior high school?

Yes. This tool can help both college and senior high school students estimate their weighted average.
